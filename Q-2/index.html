About the Department

The Department of Computer Science provides quality education in computer science, programming, software development, and emerging technologies. The department encourages students to develop technical, analytical, and problem-solving skills.

Programs Offered

  • Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science
  • Master of Technology in Computer Science
  • Ph.D. in Computer Science

Vision

To become a center of excellence in computer science education, research, and innovation and prepare students for the challenges of a rapidly changing technological world.

Mission

  1. Provide strong foundations in computer science and technology.
  2. Encourage research, innovation, and practical learning.
  3. Develop professional and ethical values among students.
  4. Promote collaboration with industry and academic institutions.
